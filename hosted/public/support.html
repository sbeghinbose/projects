Extra exam time

Set this before the student starts. Extra minutes extend their personal timer, up to the exam's closing time. Set a sufficiently long submission window when scheduling.

Account recovery requests

Verify identity through your school records or in person before resetting a password. Share the new password privately. Request messages are supplied by the requester.

Encrypted backup and restore

Download accounts, questions, exams, attempts, results and settings in a password-protected backup. Sessions are excluded. Keep the backup and its passphrase safe; the passphrase cannot be recovered.

Restore an earlier backup

Restore replaces all portal data with the selected backup. Download a fresh backup first. Your current administrator password is retained, and everyone will need to sign in again.
